# Topics

The areas Discover must cover before Design starts. Status is `open`, `in progress` or
`covered`. Add product-specific topics as new rows; keep the ids short and lowercase.

| id | Topic | Status | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| vision | Vision and positioning | open | Problem, promise, differentiation against alternatives |
| users | Users | open | Personas, first audience, needs, who it is not for |
| core-loop | Core loop | open | What people do repeatedly and why they come back |
| features | Features | open | Inventory, scope per release, acceptance criteria |
| journeys | User journeys | open | Every main flow step by step, including first use |
| data | Data and integrations | open | Sources, sync, ownership, domain model |
| privacy | Privacy and security | open | What is collected, stored, shared; accounts; threats |
| platforms | Platforms | open | Devices, release order, offline behaviour |
| experience | Brand and experience | open | Look, tone, motion, sound, accessibility |
| business | Business and distribution | open | Pricing, licence, stores, launch criteria |
| quality | Non-functional needs | open | Performance, reliability, localisation, support |
| architecture | Architecture | open | Components, technology, hosting, decisions as ADRs |
| risks | Risks and legal | open | What could sink it; licences; dependencies |
